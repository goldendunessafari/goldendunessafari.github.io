When to Visit the Abu Dhabi Desert

A month-by-month look at what the weather actually does, and how it changes the trip.

The short answer is October to March. The longer answer is more useful, because the best month depends on what you are going for — photographs, a barbecue under the stars, or simply not being uncomfortable.

The quick version

MonthsWhat it is likeWorth knowing
October – NovemberWarm days, cool eveningsThe most comfortable window of the year. Busy, so book earlier than you think.
December – FebruaryMild days, genuinely cold nightsTake a jacket. Morning fog is common and occasionally delays pickups.
March – AprilWarming up fastStill good. Evening trips start to make more sense than mornings.
May – SeptemberHot, then very hotEvening and overnight trips only. Mornings are hard work.

Winter: the fog nobody mentions

Between December and February the Abu Dhabi hinterland gets thick early-morning fog, sometimes down to a few metres of visibility on the roads east of the city. It usually clears by nine or ten. It is the one weather event that regularly pushes a morning pickup back, and it is not a sign of a badly run trip — no operator drives a convoy into that.

The upside of the same months: evenings at the camp are cool enough to sit outside comfortably, and the sand is pleasant to walk on barefoot at sunset.

Rain

Rain in the UAE is rare and brief, mostly in January and February. A light shower changes little. Heavy rain is different: wet sand behaves differently under a vehicle and access tracks can flood, so trips are occasionally postponed rather than run badly. If it looks like rain on your date, message ahead rather than assuming.

Summer, honestly

From late May the middle of the day in the dunes is punishing, and by July and August even the evening carries heat off the sand for the first hour. Trips still run and plenty of people enjoy them, but go in knowing what you are choosing. If summer is your only option, take the latest departure available and drink far more water than feels necessary.

Ramadan and public holidays

During Ramadan, timings shift: evening trips typically start later so that the camp meal falls after sunset. Eid and National Day weekends are the busiest dates of the year in the desert, and both the camps and the roads out feel it.

So which month

If you can choose freely, aim for November or February: comfortable temperatures, clear evenings, and neither the peak-season crush of late December nor the heat that arrives in April.

Whatever month you land on, confirm the current pickup times before you plan the rest of the day, since they move with sunset through the year. The current schedule is published here.
